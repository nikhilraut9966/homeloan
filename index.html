Calculation assumptions

  • Dropline OD principal/limit reduces in equal monthly instalments: original loan amount ÷ total months.
  • Dropline OD monthly principal repayment is always the original sanctioned loan amount divided by the selected tenure, irrespective of whether an OD deposit is maintained.
  • The average OD deposit is assumed to be available from Day 1 and maintained throughout the tenure. It offsets the outstanding principal only for interest calculation; it does not reduce the scheduled monthly principal repayment.
  • OD interest is approximated monthly as max(0, opening OD principal − average deposit) × OD ROI ÷ 12. Actual bank OD interest is generally based on daily outstanding balances and the bank's sanctioned drawing-power schedule.
  • Regular loan uses a standard reducing-balance EMI at the regular ROI.
  • Taxes, insurance, processing fees, rate resets, payment dates and other charges are excluded. This is an illustrative comparison, not an official Bank of Baroda quotation or confirmation of product terms.
